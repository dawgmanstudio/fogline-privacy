Fogline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Fogline (the "Game"), developed and published by Raphael Havia ("I", "me"), handles your information. In short: the Game does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Information I collect

None. The Game does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, and it does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. I do not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, location, or any other personal details to play.

Information stored on your device

Any game settings or progress are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to me or to anyone else, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Third-party services

The Game does not include third-party analytics or advertising services. If you download the Game through the Apple App Store or Google Play, those platforms may collect information under their own privacy policies, which I do not control:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's privacy

Because the Game does not collect any personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children of any age.

Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features, I will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above.
