Fogline Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Fogline (the "Game"), developed and published by Raphael Havia ("I", "me"), handles your information.

In short: I do not collect or store any personal information myself. The Game shows advertisements through Unity Ads, a third-party service operated by Unity Technologies, which collects some device and usage information in order to deliver ads.

Information I collect

None. I do not ask you to create an account, and I do not collect, receive, or store your name, email address, or any other personal details. The Game does not use any analytics service of its own.

Information stored on your device

Game settings and progress are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to me, and it is removed when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The Game displays ads using the Unity Ads SDK. When ads are shown, Unity Technologies and its partners may collect information such as:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID (IDFA on iOS, Google Advertising ID on Android);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information, such as model, operating system, language, screen size, and connection type;
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them, and general app usage;
  • diagnostic information related to ad delivery.

This information is used by Unity to deliver and measure ads, show ads that may be relevant to you, and prevent fraud. It is collected and processed by Unity under its own privacy policy, not by me, and I do not have access to it in a form that identifies you:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Your choices about ads

  • Personalized ads: you can opt out of personalized ads at any time by tapping the data privacy icon shown on Unity ads, or through your device settings ("Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iOS, or "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).
  • Consent: where required by law (for example, in the European Union or the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. On iOS, the Game will ask for permission before your advertising identifier is used for tracking.
  • Remove ads: you can remove ads entirely with the one-time "No Ads" in-app purchase.
  • Your data held by Unity: to access or delete information Unity has collected, follow the instructions in Unity's privacy policy linked above.

In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I do not receive or store your payment details. These purchases are subject to the privacy policies of those platforms:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under the age of 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact me and I will help you address it with Unity.

Changes to this policy

If the Game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding online features or other third-party services, I will update this policy and change the "Last updated" date above.
